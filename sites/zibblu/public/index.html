03 / PRIVATE MODE

Your network. Your moment.

Start a separate, opt-in, encrypted chat with people on a reachable local network. No internet fallback and no persistent private-session history in the app.

LOCAL-FIRST, BY DESIGN

Your memories belong on your devices.

Zibblu uses the server to deliver messages and temporary attachments, not as a permanent archive of your conversations. Messages you've received live in your local chat history.

This also means a brand-new device cannot automatically restore years of old chats from the server. Keep a copy of important local data.

The server retains account, contact, group and device records. Expired online content is processed by periodic cleanup; backups may have separate retention windows.

CONTROL THE CONTEXT

Privacy should be a choice you understand.

Private LAN chats are a distinct mode: participants agree to join, messages use direct encrypted local connections, and the app clears private session content when the session ends.

Good to know: regular online chats are not end-to-end encrypted. Private-session cleanup cannot erase screenshots, copies, or operating-system remnants.

AI, on your terms.

When you ask for AI help, you choose the provider and which supported messages or files it can read for that request. No AI access to private LAN chats.
