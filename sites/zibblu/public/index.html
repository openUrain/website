02 — Private LAN

Some things
stay between us.

When you're on a reachable local network, you can invite another verified device into a separate encrypted text session. It travels directly between devices—never through a message relay.

Understand private chat ↗
A CLEAR DISTINCTION

Ordinary chats use your selected online server and are not end-to-end encrypted. Private LAN text is a separate, opt-in mode with no internet fallback; ending it clears app-managed in-memory text, not screenshots or OS traces.

03 — Your assistant

Ask about what matters.

Mention a configured AI assistant in an ordinary chat. Choose the messages and optional files for a request, then review what will go to your selected provider. The assistant is read-only. Private LAN content stays out of cloud AI.
