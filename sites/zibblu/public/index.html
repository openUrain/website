Two ways to chat.

Your infrastructure

Enterprise Self-Hosting

Deploy Zibblu Online and PostgreSQL with Docker Compose. Your team signs in to your HTTPS server, which delivers internal messages and files without routing them through official Zibblu servers.

Deployment guide on GitHub ↗ Zibblu repository access required.
LAN only

Private LAN Chat

Participants opt in to a separate text session and their devices verify each other. An encrypted direct connection carries messages across a reachable LAN, with no server relay or internet fallback.

Security details on GitHub ↗ Zibblu repository access required.

A separate space for private chat.

Open a private invitation from a conversation or from Nearby devices. Once accepted, it opens in a dedicated dark view, separate from your ordinary chat history.

The account service issues credentials for device verification. Private text stays on the direct connection.

Everyday chat, built around your device.

Local-first history

Received ordinary chats stay in SQLite on the device that received them. Browse that history when an active session goes offline. A new device can receive eligible pending deliveries and new messages.

File sharing

Send files and folders, resume interrupted transfers and verify completed downloads. Your server’s policy determines how long a copy is available to download.

Read-only AI

Mention your assistant in an ordinary chat. Review the provider, selected messages and optional text files before sending each request. Cloud AI has no access to private LAN chats.
